Institutional Profile

Mueeniyya Garden of Qur'an Studies For Girls is an educational institution established to help girls memorize the Qur'an and shape an Islamic lifestyle. A carefully designed syllabus and the supervision of experienced teachers ensure teaching excellence. The best technological and lifestyle facilities help students acquire deep comprehension skills and correct Tajweed knowledge through an integrated learning method.

Along with academic excellence, special training to develop non-academic skills such as multilingual learning, writing, personality development, motivation sessions, career guidance, and fests are also integral components of the syllabus. The aim of the institution is to shape students who achieve integrated growth in knowledge and character.
